    Diammonium phosphate is the phosphate fertilizer Afghan wheat programs rely on at sowing. We supply it in 15, 25 and 50 kg bags to the grade your tender specifies.

    Ask for a DAP quoteUse the quote form

    About DAP

    DAP supplies both phosphorus and nitrogen in one granule. Phosphorus builds strong roots and early growth, which is why it is applied when the crop is sown.

    Good DAP comes in dark, brown or yellow grades — hard, evenly sized and dry. It should pour freely from the bag without dust or lumps.

    Typical specification

    Total nitrogen (N)18% min
    Available phosphate (P₂O₅)46% min
    Moisture2.0% max
    Granule size2 to 4 mm, 90% min
    ColourDark, brown or yellow
    Packing15, 25 or 50 kg woven bags with inner liner

    Typical values. The exact specification is written on each quotation and certificate of analysis.

    When it is used

    • Winter wheat: worked into the soil at sowing in autumn.
    • Spring crops such as maize, rice and vegetables: applied at planting.
    • Application rates follow MAIL and DAIL recommendations for the crop and province.

    Urea fertilizer, 46% nitrogen.

    Urea carries more nitrogen than any other solid fertilizer. We supply granular or prilled urea in 15, 25 and 50 kg bags for top dressing programs.

    Ask for a Urea quoteUse the quote form
    46N0P₂O₅0K₂O
    The highest nitrogen of any solid fertilizer

    About Urea

    Nitrogen drives leaf growth, tillering and grain filling. Urea is applied while the crop is growing, usually in one or two splits.

    Good urea is white, dry and free-flowing, with low biuret so it does not harm young plants.

    Typical specification

    Total nitrogen (N)46% min
    Biuret1.0% max
    Moisture0.5% max
    FormGranular 2 to 4 mm, or prilled
    ColourWhite
    Packing15, 25 or 50 kg woven bags with inner liner

    Typical values. The exact specification is written on each quotation and certificate of analysis.

    When it is used

    • Winter wheat: top dressing in spring at tillering and before stem elongation.
    • Maize and rice: split applications during the growing season.
    • Application rates follow MAIL and DAIL recommendations for the crop and province.

        Integrity and compliance

        • We follow your procurement procedures, deadlines and submission format.
        • We sign your supplier code of conduct and conflict of interest declarations.
        • No gifts, commissions or side payments, ever.
        • Payment by bank transfer against invoice, as set out in the contract.
        Can you provide samples before we award?

        Yes. Tell us how many samples you need and where to deliver them, and we will arrange it within your evaluation period.

        Do you deliver to remote districts?

        Yes. We quote delivery to the district or village point you specify, and state any road or season limits on the quotation.

        Can bags carry our program marking?

        Yes. Send the marking artwork with your RFQ so it is included in the price and the delivery period.

        How long are quotations valid?

        For the period your RFQ requires. Fertilizer prices move with world markets, so the validity date is always written on the quotation.

        Which documents do you need from us?

        Your RFQ or bill of quantities, the delivery location and the required delivery date. Technical specifications and marking requirements help us quote exactly.

        HomeHow we work

        How an order moves, from request to handover.

        Every step is confirmed in writing, so your team always knows what was agreed and where the goods are.

        1. 1

          Send your request

          Send your RFQ, bill of quantities or a simple list by WhatsApp or email. Include the delivery location and the date you need the goods.

        2. 2

          Get a quotation

          We reply with a signed quotation that you can file with your bid evaluation.

          • Unit price and total, with validity date
          • Specification, origin and packing
          • Present stock and delivery period
        3. 3

          Inspect samples

          Your technical team checks samples and documents before award. Independent laboratory analysis is arranged on request.

        4. 4

          Contract and purchase order

          We sign your contract and confirm the delivery schedule, marking and documents you need.

        5. 5

          Loading and transport

          Bags are counted, checked and loaded onto sealed trucks. You receive updates on WhatsApp until the goods arrive.

        6. 6

          Handover

          Your team counts and inspects on site and signs the delivery note. We then send the invoice and the full set of documents.

        Season calendar

        When fertilizer is applied across most of Afghanistan. Order at least one to two months before the window opens.

        Timing shifts with altitude and province. Highland areas sow earlier in autumn and later in spring.

        Main entry points for imports

        Afghanistan imports fertilizer and food overland. We choose the route by product, origin and destination province.

        • Hairatan, from Uzbekistan
        • Torghundi and Aqina, from Turkmenistan
        • Sher Khan Bandar, from Tajikistan
        • Islam Qala, from Iran
        • Torkham and Spin Boldak, from Pakistan

        Checks before every dispatch

        • Grade and appearance against the quotation
        • Bag weight spot checks and bag condition
        • Moisture, caking and dust for fertilizer
        • Production and expiry dates for food
        • Truck count, seals and documents
